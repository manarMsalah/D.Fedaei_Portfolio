Executive Summary

A Career Defined by
Excellence in Kidney Medicine

Professor Fedaei Mohamed El-Sayed Abbas is one of the Arab world's foremost authorities in clinical nephrology, hemodialysis, and organ transplantation, with a career spanning more than four decades across Egypt and Kuwait.

Graduating from Al-Azhar University in 1981, Professor Abbas pursued a rigorous academic and clinical pathway that culminated in a Doctorate in Internal Medicine and Nephrology in 1997 and a Master's degree with Distinction in Organ Transplantation Science from the University of Liverpool, United Kingdom. His commitment to continual professional development was further recognised when he received the Leadership Prize from the Global Kidney Academy (GKA), London, in 2015.

Over the course of his career, Professor Abbas has served as Head of the Nephrology and Hemodialysis Department at two of the region's most prominent medical institutions: Ahmed Maher Teaching Hospital, Cairo and Jaber El-Ahmed Military Hospital, Kuwait, where he was instrumental in establishing and developing the department from the ground up between 2006 and 2021.

A prolific researcher, he has authored over 25 peer-reviewed articles in nationally and internationally indexed journals, contributed to landmark transplantation literature published in the World Journal of Transplantation, and authored a 794-question reference textbook. He is an Editor of the Journal of Renal Transplantation Science (Sci Tech Central Inc., USA) and a Member of the Scientific Committee of the ASNRT Fellowship Programme in Clinical Transplantation.

Procedural Expertise

Clinical Skills &
Invasive Procedures

Renal Biopsy

Ultrasound-guided percutaneous renal biopsy for histopathological diagnosis of native and transplant kidney disease. Extensive experience across academic and military hospital settings.

Femoral Venous Catheterisation

Femoral catheter placement for acute hemodialysis access - a critical life-saving intervention in acute kidney injury and emergency renal replacement therapy.

Internal Jugular Venous Catheterisation

Internal jugular central venous catheter placement for acute hemodialysis, providing reliable upper-body vascular access in inpatient and intensive care settings.

Subclavian Venous Catheterisation

Subclavian catheter placement for hemodialysis access in selected cases requiring alternative or long-term central venous access, with meticulous attention to minimising complications.

Tunnelled Permcath Catheter Insertion

Placement of right- and left-sided tunnelled (Permcath) catheters for long-term hemodialysis access - a technically demanding procedure requiring precise anatomical knowledge.

Hemodialysis Unit Management

Full operational leadership of hemodialysis units - establishment, staffing, protocol development, quality assurance, and supervision of complex dialysis patients at both Ahmed Maher and Jaber El-Ahmed Hospitals.
